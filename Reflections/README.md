# Learning Reflections — Evidence Index

**M9: Cloud Operations and Management | MSc Cybersecurity | University of Essex Online | October 2026**

## Purpose

This index presents five individual learning reflections prepared during the Cloud Operations and Management module. Together, they demonstrate the development of my cloud computing knowledge, technical skills, teamwork and professional judgement.

These are selected reflections from Units 1, 2, 3, 11 and 12, rather than a claim that a separate reflection was produced for every unit.

## Reflection Records

| Unit | Reflection Focus | Main Evidence of Learning |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Cloud fundamentals and service models** | Reflection on previous Azure experience, cloud characteristics, deployment models, shared responsibility and introductory automation. |
| 02 | **Team communication and coordination** | Reflection on improving communication during a group project, addressing delays and respecting different collaboration preferences. |
| 03 | **IaaS, PaaS and SaaS** | Comparison of cloud service models, including the balance between customer control, provider responsibilities and organisational needs. |
| 11 | **TensorFlow, Keras and Azure image recognition** | Technical reflection supported by five evidence figures, covering CNN training, FastAPI, Docker, Azure Container Apps deployment and scale-to-zero behaviour. |
| 12 | **Ethical, social and professional responsibilities** | Reflection on UK GDPR, secure cloud migration, recovery objectives, vendor dependency, sustainability, least privilege and Zero Trust. |
