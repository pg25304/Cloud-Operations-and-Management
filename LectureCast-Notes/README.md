# LectureCast Evidence Index

**M9: Cloud Operations and Management | MSc Cybersecurity | University of Essex Online | October 2026**

## Purpose

This index records six LectureCast study documents prepared during the module. They bring together lecture material, explanatory notes, comparisons and selected diagrams used for revision. The associated practical projects are listed separately to show how some of the subjects were explored through hands-on work.

> **Access and attribution:** The original Word documents are retained in the private university OneDrive workspace. Some contain lecturer wording, diagrams or screenshots from the learning platform. The originals should not be uploaded to a public repository without permission and a content review. This public index is a navigation record, not a substitute for those materials.
